2. Solve the simultaneous equations: 2x + y = 7 and x - y = 2. Find the values of x and y.
[Answer: x = 3, y = 1. (2(3) + 1 = 7, 3 - 1 = 2)]

4. Calculate the compound interest on N10,000 for 2 years at 5% per annum. (Show your workings.)
[Answer: Interest Year 1 = 5% of 10,000 = 500; Amount Year 1 = 10,500. Interest Year 2 = 5% of 10,500 = 525; Total Interest = 500 + 525 = N1,025. Total Amount = N11,025.]

4. Identify the figure of speech in the sentence: “The wind whispered through the trees.” Explain your answer.
[Answer: The figure of speech is personification – the wind is given the human quality of whispering, suggesting a soft, gentle sound.]

1. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?
A. Sunlight
B. Crude oil
C. Wind
D. Water
[Answer: B, Marks: 10]

2. Which organelle is known as the “powerhouse of the cell”?
A. Nucleus
B. Ribosome
C. Mitochondria
D. Golgi body
[Answer: C, Marks: 10]

3. Name and explain three functions of the human skeleton.
[Answer: 1. Support – Provides a framework that supports the body and maintains its shape. 2. Protection – Protects vital organs such as the brain (skull), heart and lungs (ribcage). 3. Movement – Acts as levers for muscles to pull against, enabling locomotion and manipulation.]
[Marks: 20]
[Lines: 6]

4. Describe the process of photosynthesis, including the raw materials and products.
[Answer: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to synthesise food from carbon dioxide and water. Raw materials: Carbon dioxide (CO₂), Water (H₂O), and Sunlight (energy). Products: Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) and Oxygen (O₂). The equation is: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. It occurs in the chloroplasts, which contain chlorophyll.]
[Marks: 25]
[Lines: 8]
